Read <a href="/research/h1b-60-day-grace-period-layoff-guide">how the H-1B 60-day grace period is calculated</a>. This page covers EB-5 options after an H-1B layoff. For the pathway page, see <a href="/pathways/h1b-60-day-clock">EB-5 options after an H-1B layoff</a>. You can apply for EB-5 during H-1B grace period by filing Form I-526E and, when your priority date is current, concurrently filing Form I-485 with applications for an EAD and Advance Parole, which is often the cleanest way to avoid deportation after H-1B layoff. The question of can I stay in USA after H-1B layoff if I apply for EB-5 generally turns on filing a timely change of status or adjustment of status before day 60, which preserves lawful presence while your case is pending. Because EB-5 is investor-driven, the h1b grace period eb5 strategy does not require a new employer, giving you control over your timeline and the freedom to work for any employer once your EAD is issued. As an emergency immigration strategy USA professionals increasingly rely on, EB-5 also protects your spouse and unmarried children under 21 under the same petition, provided your $800,000 investment is at risk in a qualifying TEA project and your source of funds is fully documented. Consult a qualified immigration attorney for advice specific to your situation.
